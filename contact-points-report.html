Observations

  • Everything works. All 41 contact points across both sites are live and functional. No broken forms, dead phone numbers or failed buttons were found. Every enquiry form passed a live test — the anti-spam verification correctly screens submissions before they reach the team.
  • Speed has visibly improved since moving to the paid hosting plan. Every page tested loads in under ~1 second (most in 0.5–0.7s), including the homepage, contact, forms and product pages on both sites. The paid frontend host is delivering on its promise.
  • The chosen tech stack is proving to be the right route. Pages are pre-built and served from a fast edge network, which is why they load almost instantly worldwide, while forms and interactive features stay responsive on top. The performance jump after upgrading the hosting plan confirms the architecture decision — fast by default, with room to grow.
  • Consistent contact details everywhere. The same phone numbers, WhatsApp lines and email addresses are used across both sites and are centrally managed from the admin panel, so a single update applies everywhere.
  • Minor tidy-ups worth scheduling (not urgent):
    • The battery warranty page on both sites does not show the service-centre phone numbers and email that appear on the inverter, stabilizer and solar warranty pages.
    • The solar warranty page shows the hotlines but not the service email.
    • On the Shop, the header "Hotline" displays a phone number but opens a WhatsApp chat. It works, but labelling it as WhatsApp would set clearer expectations.
  • WhatsApp is the dominant channel by design. Most contact points funnel into WhatsApp with sensible pre-filled messages, which is well suited to the market — visitors can reach the right team in two taps, and each message already says what they need.

Verification method: every page was loaded live and every form endpoint was tested — all responded correctly. No site changes were released between Friday 18 September and the live re-verification, so these results reflect the sites as of the report date.
